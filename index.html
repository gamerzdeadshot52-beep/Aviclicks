About Us

A studio built around feeling, not just frames.

AVI CLICKS is a wedding photography brand led by Avinash — a photographer who believes the best pictures are the ones you can feel long after the day has passed. Based in Lucknow, India, and available worldwide, we document weddings the way they actually happen: the laughter, the quiet glances, the tears nobody planned for.

Every wedding has its own rhythm. We work gently and stay observant, so you can be fully present with the people you love while we quietly preserve it — honest, elegant, and unmistakably yours.
